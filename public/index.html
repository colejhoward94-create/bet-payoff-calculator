Definitions

EV (expected value)
The average profit or loss per bet if you could place the exact same bet many times, using your hit-chance estimates. It’s a long-run average, not what happens on any single bet.
EV %
EV as a percent of the amount staked. +8% means you’d expect to make 8¢ per $1 risked over time. −10% means you’d expect to lose 10¢ per $1. Example: three 60% legs on a 5x power play hit 21.6% of the time, so 0.216 × 5 = 1.08 returned per $1, which is +8% EV.
Chance every leg hits
The odds all legs win at once. For independent legs, it’s the leg probabilities multiplied together.
Chance of profit
The odds you get back more than you staked. On flex plays this includes partial hits that pay above 1x.
Break-even per leg
The hit chance each leg needs for the bet to have 0% EV. If your estimates are above it, the bet has an edge. If below, the payout isn’t enough to cover the risk.
Edge per leg
Your average leg hit chance minus the break-even per leg, in percentage points.
Payout multiple
Total return per $1 staked, stake included. A 5x payout on $10 returns $50 ($40 profit). Entered as hits:multiple, like 3:5.
Power vs. Flex
Power plays need every pick to hit for a bigger payout. Flex plays pay smaller amounts for some misses.
Kelly criterion
A formula for the stake size that grows a bankroll fastest given your edge. Full Kelly is aggressive and swings a lot, so most people use a fraction of it.
Kelly fraction
The share of full Kelly you actually use. 0.25 means a quarter of it.
Bankroll
The total money you’ve set aside for betting.
Bonus funds
Promo money instead of your own cash. A win usually pays the profit only, not the stake back, so you can’t lose real money on the entry. The app shows expected cash back per $1 of bonus instead of EV.
Boosted payout
A temporary higher multiple offered by the app. The calculator compares EV with the normal payout and the boosted one so you can see what the boost is worth.
Refund offer
Some promos return your stake as bonus credit if you lose. It’s counted at the credit value you set in Settings, since credit is worth less than cash.
Matchup adjustment
An optional per-leg read. Marking a matchup favorable or unfavorable moves that leg’s hit chance up or down by the number of points set in Settings. Leave it on Unknown if you don’t have a read.
Seriousness slider
Blends two ways of sizing a bet. Toward “Just for fun,” the stake limit comes from your max-stake caps and the verdict thresholds relax. Toward “Serious,” it uses Kelly sizing and your full criteria.
Estimate haircut
A conservative cut applied to every hit-chance estimate, since estimates are often too optimistic.
Correlation
When legs depend on each other, like two players in the same game. This calculator assumes legs are independent, so correlated legs make the real odds differ from the displayed ones.
Fair probability
The hit chance sportsbooks are pricing, with their built-in margin removed. The app averages every book quoting that exact line, and counts Pinnacle (the sharpest book) double. If no book has the exact line on the DFS app, it uses the nearest book line and says so. It’s the market’s view, so beating it takes a real edge.
Implied probability
The hit chance a payout implies. A 2x payout on a single pick implies 50%.
Gut-check math only, not financial or betting advice. Results are only as good as the probabilities you put in. Bet only what you can afford to lose.
